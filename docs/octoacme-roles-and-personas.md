# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They work with Product and Development teams to define acceptance criteria, design test approaches, and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for the project
- Create and maintain acceptance criteria aligned with Definition of Done
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end test plans
- Identify and log defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate fixes and perform regression testing
- Coordinate security and performance testing
- Report quality metrics to the team and PM

### Goals
- Catch defects early to reduce cost of fixes
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Build team confidence in quality through transparent metrics

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA strategy documents
- Defect reports and quality metrics
- Collaboration with developers on acceptance criteria
- QA reviews and sign-off meetings

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide design and architectural decisions for the project. They provide technical mentorship, ensure scalability and maintainability, and help the team navigate complex technical challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review technical approach for scalability and risk
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure code quality and architecture standards are met
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Build team capability through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and architectural guidance
- Technical mentoring sessions
- Risk assessments and trade-off analysis

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They help the team continuously improve and maintain a healthy, productive workflow.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Track sprint metrics and burndown
- Escalate organizational barriers to the PM or Product Lead
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Maintain team velocity and predictability
- Reduce cycle time and improve delivery cadence
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony facilitation and participation
- Blocker resolution and escalation
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Metrics and velocity reports

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight across multiple products, projects, or teams. They align Product Managers and stakeholders on strategic priorities, resolve cross-team conflicts, and ensure investments deliver business value.

### Responsibilities
- Align product strategy across multiple projects or teams
- Escalate strategic decisions and conflicts
- Review product roadmap priorities and trade-offs
- Represent product needs to executives and stakeholders
- Mentor Product Managers
- Ensure portfolio alignment with business strategy

### Goals
- Ensure portfolio alignment with business strategy
- Maximize cross-team efficiency and reduce conflicts
- Drive product outcomes at scale

### Typical Communication
- Strategic planning and roadmap reviews
- Portfolio prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Executive stakeholder briefings
- Product Manager mentoring and alignment

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions advise on security requirements, threat modeling, and compliance throughout the project lifecycle. They integrate security early and ensure risks are identified and mitigated.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements aligned with organizational standards
- Conduct threat modeling and security design reviews
- Guide security testing and vulnerability scanning
- Advise on data protection and compliance requirements
- Escalate security risks and coordinate remediation
- Build security awareness and best practices within the team

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Integrate security early in the development lifecycle
- Build team security awareness and capability

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement specifications and threat models
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Compliance and regulatory guidance
- Security training and awareness sessions

---

## Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business owners and executives who fund and champion the project. They provide strategic direction, remove organizational barriers, and serve as the escalation point for business-impacting decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and investment
- Serve as escalation point for strategic decisions
- Remove organizational barriers and competing priorities
- Provide executive visibility and support
- Review and approve major scope or timeline changes
- Attend key milestone reviews and retrospectives
- Advocate for the project and secure resources

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Reduce delays from organizational friction
- Build organizational alignment on priorities

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and charter reviews
- Milestone and phase gate approvals
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Escalation resolution and sponsorship

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are external or cross-functional partners who provide input, approvals, and receive status updates. They represent customer needs, dependencies, or organizational interests in the project.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain expertise and requirements input
- Review and approve deliverables (if needed)
- Participate in design and planning reviews
- Provide feedback on user experience and fit
- Communicate status to their own teams or departments

### Goals
- Ensure project addresses their needs and concerns
- Reduce rework from misaligned requirements
- Enable successful adoption and integration

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering and feedback sessions
- Status updates and milestone reviews
- Acceptance and sign-off on deliverables
- Integration planning and handoff meetings
